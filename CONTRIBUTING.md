# Corrections and additions

This registry improves by being checked. Corrections are welcome from anyone, including developers of listed molecules.

**To propose a correction or addition,** [open an issue using the correction template](../../issues/new?template=correction.md) and include:
- the molecule and the field you think is wrong (or the asset you think is missing);
- what it should say;
- a source: registry record, peer-reviewed paper, regulatory document or dated company statement.

**How it's handled.** Each proposal is checked against its source and the written rules in `METHODOLOGY.md`. Accepted changes go into the next release, logged in `CHANGELOG.md` with the date and the evidence. Declined proposals get a reason in the issue.

**Developers:** if your molecule is listed as borderline or excluded and you can document a molecule-level AI role, send the documentation. If you think it shouldn't be listed at all, say why.

Please don't send confidential information. Everything here is built from public sources.
