AI Molecules in the Clinic

An open registry of drug candidates whose target or molecule was materially shaped by AI, and which have been dosed in humans. Counted to a written definition, sourced row by row, open to correction.

Strict cohort only. Precedented share is of molecules with a disclosed target. Borderline entries are listed but not counted.

What counts

Material AI roleMachine learning or generative methods did real work choosing the target or designing the molecule.
Attributed to this moleculeThe developer or a first-tier source says so for this asset, not just for the company's platform.
Dosed in humansIn any registry or per a dated announcement. Borderline cases are published with the reason they miss.

Read before citing

  • Attribution is mostly self-reported. 53 of 54 strict entries rest on the developer's own statement that AI was used.
  • Not yet second-coded. Target-precedence calls follow a written rule but were made by one coder. Independent second coding is planned.
  • The count is a range. About 54, −7/+8, depending on registry coverage and on whether physics-based design counts.
  • Inclusion is not an efficacy rating. Being listed says nothing about whether a molecule works.
  • Entries were assembled with AI-assisted searches and challenged by an adversarial verification pass. Every row carries its sources.
