AI Molecules in the Clinic
Registry The question Methodology Corrections

49 AI-derived molecules have been dosed in people. None is approved.

This is an open registry of drug candidates whose target or molecule was materially shaped by AI or machine learning. Each one is counted to a written definition, sourced row by row, and open to correction.

Most have gone after biology the industry already knew how to hit: 33 of the 47 with a disclosed target. For all but one (rentosertib), the only evidence that AI did the work is the developer's own statement.

What counts

A molecule is in the strict cohort only if all three hold. Entries that miss one test in a way reasonable people dispute are published as borderline, with the reason, but not counted. The full definition is in Methodology § 1.

  • AI did material work

    Machine learning or generative methods helped choose the target or design the molecule, not just run the screening logistics.

  • For this molecule

    The developer or a first-tier source attributes that role to this asset, not just to the company's platform in general.

  • Dosed in humans

    In any trial registry worldwide, or per a dated developer announcement, by the as-of date.

How targets are classified

Each molecule's target is judged as it stood on the day the molecule was first dosed in a person. The question is how much the field already knew about hitting that target in people. The rule is set out in Methodology § 3; each entry shows the basis for its call.

  • Known target

    An approved drug, or another drug that had reached Phase 2 or later, already acted on this target. The biology was validated in patients before the AI molecule arrived.

  • Novel

    Other drugs had been dosed in people at this target, but none had got past Phase 1. Known to be druggable, not yet shown to matter in patients.

  • First-in-class

    No drug had ever been dosed in people at this target. This is the case that would show AI finding new biology, not just new chemistry.

  • Undisclosed

    The developer has not said what the target is, so it cannot be classified.

Why other counts are bigger

Published counts of AI-discovered drugs in the clinic range from about 50 to more than 100. They differ mainly in what they ask.

A lower number is not more correct. Each count answers a different question, and this one asks the narrowest. The uncertainty band on 49 is about −2 to +8, depending on registry coverage and on whether physics-based design counts. How the band is built.
